# Daily Water Intake Body Weight Calculator

Enter your body weight (kg or lb), activity level, and climate to get a personalized daily water intake recommendation in ml, liters, and 8 oz cups. Log each drink with preset buttons (250 ml, 350 ml, 500 ml bottle, 1 cup) or a custom amount, and track your progress on an animated circular ring that resets automatically at midnight.

## Features

- Personalized recommendation formula based on weight, activity multiplier, and climate factor
- Animated progress ring with color shift (amber → blue → green) as you reach your goal
- Quick-add buttons for common drink sizes plus a custom ml input
- Daily intake log with timestamps, persisted in localStorage and auto-cleared each new day
- Auto-restores your target and today's entries on page reload
